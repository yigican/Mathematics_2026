# Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

- $A$ is $2\times3$ and $B$ is $3\times2$.
- $a_{12}=-1$, $a_{23}=5$, $b_{21}=-2$, and $b_{32}=1$.
The second row of $A$ is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The first column of $B$ is

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**Method and check:** The first subscript identifies the row, and the second identifies the column. For instance, $b_{32}$ is the entry in the third row and second column of $B$, which is $1$. Counting the displayed rows and entries per row also confirms the dimensions.
