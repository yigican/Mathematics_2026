# Exercise 8. A Square Root in a Function Limit

## Question

Compute

$$
\lim_{x\to4}\frac{\sqrt{x}-2}{x-4}
$$

by rationalizing the numerator. Compare the idea with Exercise 3 involving a sequence.

> **Why this exercise:** reinforces transferring the same algebraic technique between two types of limits.

## Solution

Rationalize:

$$
\frac{\sqrt{x}-2}{x-4}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{x}+2}{\sqrt{x}+2}
=\frac1{\sqrt{x}+2}\quad(x\neq4).
$$

The limit is $1/4$. As in Exercise 3, multiplying by the conjugate resolves a $0/0$ or $\infty-\infty$ indeterminate expression by exploiting a difference of squares.
